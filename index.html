The route of an order

From our shelves to your door.

No dropshipping, no marketplace. We own what we sell, and every parcel follows the same short path.

  1. 01 — Sourcing

    Partner workshops

    Finished goods from a small group of partner workshops in Europe and Asia, checked against our specifications before they ship to us.

  2. 02 — Our stock

    Inventory we own

    We buy inventory outright. Every product on this site is physically in stock and owned by Lizom Pro Ltd.

  3. 03 — EU fulfilment

    Picked and packed in the EU

    Stock is stored at third-party fulfilment centres inside the European Union, where orders are picked, packed and handed to carriers.

  4. 04 — Your door

    Tracked delivery

    Tracked delivery in 2 to 5 business days across the EU. If it is not right, you have 14 days to send it back.

How we operate

Built like a company, not a storefront.

Five things that stay true whether you are a customer, a partner or a bank checking that we are who we say we are.

01 / 05 Registered · England and Wales
02 / 05 Specifications · Batch checks
03 / 05 Inventory · Owned outright
04 / 05 Fulfilment · Inside the EU
05 / 05 Payments · Policies published
  1. 01

    Registered and accountable

    Lizom Pro Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales. Our company number, registered office and contact email are on every page of this site, and our directors are listed on the public Companies House register.

  2. 02

    Sourcing on our terms

    We work with a small number of partner workshops and specify fabrics, formulas and packaging ourselves. Every batch is checked against those specifications before we accept it into stock.

  3. 03

    Inventory we own

    We buy stock, not just listings. Nothing on this site is dropshipped. If a product is shown, it is on our shelves and owned by Lizom Pro Ltd.

  4. 04

    Fulfilment inside the EU

    Orders are picked and packed by third-party fulfilment partners inside the European Union, so EU customers pay no import duties and parcels arrive in days, not weeks.

  5. 05

    Payments and policies you can read

    Card and wallet payments are processed by PCI DSS certified providers. We never see or store card details. Delivery, returns and privacy terms are published in full and written to be read.
