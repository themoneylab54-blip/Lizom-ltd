What to put in your message

  • An order question. Your order number, the country it was delivered to, and the sizes involved.
  • A return. Your order number and the item you want to send back. We reply the same business day with the return address and instructions.
  • A product question. The product name. For cosmetics, tell us which ingredient you are checking.
  • Partnership, wholesale or press. Your company and what you are looking for.
  • Compliance or verification. The documents you need. See the panel opposite.
